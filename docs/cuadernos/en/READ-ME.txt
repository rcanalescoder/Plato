Shot point notebooks · Plato
=============================

Three printable notebooks, one per discipline. Each opens with a cover that
explains the document, follows with an index of the round (a table of every clay
and a plan view), then one sheet per clay, and closes with the notice of use and
the licence.

Each sheet draws the scene from the shooter's eye, with the full trajectory and
five moments marked on it. Four of them fall inside the window in which the clay
is still within reach (release, early, good window, at the limit); the fifth is
deliberately outside, to show what being late costs. At each moment the sheet
tells you where the clay is and how much lead you need, in centimetres, in clay
widths and in degrees.

  plato-notebook-universal-fu1.pdf   Universal Trench, FITASC Universal 1, 25 sheets, 28 pages
  plato-notebook-olympic-issf1.pdf   Olympic Trap, ISSF Olympic 1, 15 sheets, 18 pages
  plato-notebook-robot-abt.pdf       Robot, ABT CPSA 2026, 25 sheets, 28 pages

All three were generated with the same settings (cartridge 400 m/s, flat rib, no
wind, 0.03 s delay, pattern 1 3/4 and neutral hold) so that leads are comparable
between disciplines.

IMPORTANT: the leads are valid ONLY for the settings the notebook was generated
with. They are printed in the header of every sheet, and any that are not the
simulator's start-up values appear highlighted in colour.

You can generate your own notebook, with your own settings, from the simulator:
the "Generate notebook" button in the parameters panel.

  Simulator and book: https://rcanalescoder.github.io/Plato/en/
  Project:            https://robertocanales.com/proyectos#plato

These figures are an estimate from a teaching model, not a field measurement.
They do not certify any installation or trajectory. This material does not
replace in-person training, the rules of the ground, or the help of an
instructor. The last page of each notebook carries the full notice and licence.
